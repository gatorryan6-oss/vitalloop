Two loops, one body

The sugar loop and the water loop, on one clock. They meet in the kidney: above 180 mg/dL the tubules cannot reabsorb all the filtered glucose, so sugar goes into the urine — and osmoles drag water out with them.

The single-loop tabs leave this out on purpose. The same body will not read the same here as it does on the Glucose tab, because every model leaves something out — and noticing what, and why it matters, is the point.
